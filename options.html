Dailies IRL settings

Before you start

Here's exactly what Dailies IRL does with your data:

  • Saves your settings (time zone, categories and task names) in your own Google account, using Google Apps Script. Only you can see them.
  • Creates a "Dailies IRL" list in Google Tasks and adds tasks from your settings to it. If you turn on Auto delete for a category, it deletes that category's unfinished tasks once their period is over.
  • Creates one hourly timer in your Google account, so tasks are added even when Chrome is closed.
  • Shows your email address so you know which account you're signed in with.

Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else, and nothing is sold or used for ads. You can delete everything at any time from this page.
