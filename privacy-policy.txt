DAILIES IRL - PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Last updated: October 14, 2026

Dailies IRL ("Dailies IRL", "the extension", "we", "us") is a Chrome extension
made by Krish Bharal, an independent developer. It adds tasks that you write,
picked at random, to a "Dailies IRL" list in your Google Tasks on a daily,
weekly or monthly schedule.

This policy explains what data Dailies IRL accesses, how it is used, where it
is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. It covers the
Dailies IRL Chrome extension and the Google Apps Script project that runs its
schedule. The same policy is linked from the Dailies IRL homepage
(https://krish544.com/dailies-irl), the Google sign-in (consent) screen, and the Chrome Web
Store listing.

The short version:
  - We have no servers of our own. Everything Dailies IRL stores stays in
    your own Google account.
  - We do not collect analytics, use cookies for tracking, show ads, or sell
    or share your data with anyone.
  - We, the developer, cannot see your tasks, your settings, or your email
    address.
  - You can delete everything at any time (see section 8).


1. GOOGLE PERMISSIONS (SCOPES) WE REQUEST AND WHY

When you first use Dailies IRL, Google asks you to sign in and allow these
permissions. Each one is used only for the purpose listed.

  a) Google Tasks
     (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks)
     "Create, edit, organize, and delete all your tasks."
     Used to:
       - read the names of your task lists, only to find the list called
         "Dailies IRL" (or create it if it doesn't exist);
       - add the tasks Dailies IRL picks for you to that list, with a due
         date;
       - if, and only if, you turn on "Auto delete unfinished tasks" for a
         category, delete unfinished tasks that Dailies IRL created in that
         list once their period has ended.
     Dailies IRL does not read, change or delete tasks in any of your other
     task lists, and it does not delete tasks you have completed.

  b) Run when you are not present
     (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.scriptapp)
     Used to create one hourly timer (an Apps Script "trigger") in your own
     Google account. The timer lets your tasks be added on schedule even
     when Chrome is closed. It runs as you, only works on your own data,
     and is created when you first save your settings.

  c) Your email address
     (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email)
     Used only to show which Google account you are signed in with on the
     settings page ("Signed in as ..."). It is not stored, logged or sent
     anywhere else.


2. INFORMATION DAILIES IRL ACCESSES AND STORES

  a) Your settings. These are the things you type into the settings page:
     your time zone, your "day off" and "double tasks" chances, your
     category names, schedules and counts, the task names you write, and
     which tasks are marked "Big".
     Stored in: Google Apps Script "user properties" in your own Google
     account. Only your account can read them. We cannot.

  b) Schedule records. A small record of when each category last added
     tasks (for example "2026-10-13"), so tasks are never added twice for
     the same day, week or month.
     Stored in: the same Apps Script user properties in your account.

  c) Your agreement. Which version of the data use notice on the settings
     page you agreed to, and when. Nothing is saved until you agree.
     Stored in: the same Apps Script user properties in your account, and
     in your browser's local storage on your own computer.

  d) The tasks Dailies IRL creates. These are task names from your settings,
     with due dates. Each task's notes say which category it came from (for
     example "Dailies IRL · Chores") plus a short tag, so that Auto delete
     can find it later.
     Stored in: your Google Tasks, in the "Dailies IRL" list, like any other
     task.

  e) Your unfinished Dailies IRL tasks. Only if you turn on Auto delete,
     the unfinished tasks in the "Dailies IRL" list are read when a new
     period starts, to find the ones from that category's earlier periods
     and delete them. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

  f) Your task list names. Read each time the schedule runs, only to find
     the "Dailies IRL" list. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

  g) Your email address. See section 1(c). It is not stored.

  h) Sign-in tokens. Chrome's built-in sign-in system (the chrome.identity
     API) gets and manages the access token that lets the extension talk to
     Google. Chrome keeps it in memory. Dailies IRL does not save it, log it
     or send it anywhere except to Google.

  i) Theme choice. Your System/Light/Dark choice is saved in your browser's
     local storage on your own computer. It is not sent anywhere.

Dailies IRL does NOT collect:
  - your browsing history, web page content or the tabs you visit;
  - analytics, tracking data, cookies or advertising IDs;
  - your name, contacts, location, payment details or any other personal
    information;
  - any Google data besides what is listed above.


3. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS USED

Your information is used only to provide Dailies IRL's single purpose:
adding randomly picked tasks that you wrote to your Google Tasks on the
schedule you set, and showing you your settings so you can change them.

It is never used for advertising, for building profiles about you, to
decide credit or lending, to train or improve any artificial intelligence
or machine learning model, or for any purpose unrelated to the feature
described above.


4. HOW YOUR INFORMATION MOVES

Dailies IRL has no backend server. Data only moves between:
  - the Dailies IRL extension in your Chrome browser, and
  - Google's own services, running as you: the Google Apps Script API
    (script.googleapis.com), Google Apps Script, and the Google Tasks API.

All of these connections use HTTPS (encrypted in transit). The extension
does not load or run code from the internet; the Apps Script project only
sends back data such as your settings.


5. WHO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH

We do not sell, rent, trade, transfer or disclose your Google user data or
any other information to anyone. There are no advertising networks, data
brokers, analytics providers or other third parties involved.

The only company that handles your data is Google, because Dailies IRL runs
entirely on Google's services in your own account. Google's handling of
that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We would only ever disclose information if it is required by law, and as
explained in section 6, we do not have access to your settings or tasks in
the first place.


6. WHAT THE DEVELOPER CAN SEE

Because the scheduler is a Google Apps Script project owned by the
developer, Google provides the developer with limited technical information
about how the project runs, the same as for any Apps Script project:
  - when a function ran, how long it took, and whether it succeeded or
    failed;
  - error messages from failed runs. These are written by Dailies IRL
    itself, and some can include the name of one of your categories (for
    example "Chores needs at least one task");
  - the total number of accounts that used the project. Google identifies
    users in these logs with temporary anonymous keys, not names or email
    addresses.

The developer uses this information only to find and fix bugs. Google
keeps these logs for a limited time set by Google, and they are not copied
or kept anywhere else. The developer cannot see your task names, your other
settings, your Google Tasks or your email address.

No person reads your data, except you.


7. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY

  - Your data stays inside your own Google account, protected by your
    Google sign-in. Only your account can read your Apps Script user
    properties and your Google Tasks.
  - Google encrypts this data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  - Access tokens are handled by Chrome's identity API and never saved by
    Dailies IRL.
  - We request only the permissions Dailies IRL needs to work (section 1).
  - The extension contains all of its own code, follows Chrome's Manifest V3
    rules, and does not run code downloaded from the internet.
  - Everything you send is checked by the Apps Script project before it is
    saved.

No system is perfectly secure, but because we don't run our own servers or
keep copies of your data, there is no Dailies IRL database to be breached.


8. HOW LONG DATA IS KEPT AND HOW TO DELETE IT

Your settings and schedule records are kept in your Google account until you
delete them. The tasks Dailies IRL created stay in your Google Tasks until
you (or "Auto delete", if you turn it on) delete them.

To delete your data:
  1. Open the Dailies IRL settings page and click "Delete my data". This
     removes all your settings, schedule records and agreement record from
     your Google account and turns off the hourly timer.
  2. To also remove the tasks it created, tick "Also delete the Dailies IRL
     list" before clicking "Delete my data", or delete the "Dailies IRL"
     list yourself in Google Tasks (https://tasks.google.com/).
  3. Remove Dailies IRL's access to your Google account at
     https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
  4. Uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions. This also removes
     your theme choice.

You can also see and delete the hourly timer yourself under "My Triggers"
at https://script.google.com/home/triggers.

If you would like help deleting your data, email support@krish544.com and we will
explain how. Since we cannot access your data ourselves, the steps above are
how it is deleted.

You can view all of your settings at any time on the settings page, and all
created tasks in Google Tasks.

10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Dailies IRL is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age to
have a Google account in your country) and does not knowingly collect data
from them. Using it requires your own Google account. If you believe a child
has used Dailies IRL, contact support@krish544.com and we will help remove the
data.


11. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK or California), you
may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to
object to how it is used. With Dailies IRL you can do all of this yourself:
view and change everything on the settings page, delete it as described in
section 8, and keep your tasks in Google Tasks. We do not sell or "share"
personal information for advertising. For any request or question, contact
support@krish544.com.
